### Why GoPro couldn't reuse DJI's rule

DJI chains on **file-size split cap + wall-clock `creation_time`**. Both signals fail here: the
GoPro cap isn't a constant (6349 caps at 10.8471 GiB @25 fps vs ~10.718 GiB for the 100 fps
recordings), and recording 6338 writes an **identical** `creation_time` onto both its chapters — so
the DJI wall-clock rule would have refused a real, valid pair. The camera's internal timecode is the
signal that works, and it's exact to ~1e-11 frames across all 6 corpus groups.

### The trap caught before it shipped

The 1 ms continuity slack was first derived from ffprobe's `format.duration` in the corpus CSV — but
production feeds the check `DJIClip.durationInSeconds`, i.e. **AVFoundation's `CMTime`**. Different
clocks. ffprobe's own `format` vs `video` durations already disagree by up to **0.667 ms** on this
corpus, which would have eaten most of a 1 ms budget **while every unit test still passed**, because
the fixtures carry CSV numbers rather than the production source. Re-measured all 13 non-final
chapters via `asset.load(.duration)`: AVFoundation reports the format duration **exactly**
(768.000000000 s / 2063.360000000 s at timescale 90000). Margin is real; recorded in the tolerance's
doc comment so it isn't re-derived.

### Two plan errors surfaced rather than absorbed

- The plan specified the new `SegmentMeta` fields as `let` with defaults. Swift **drops a
  `let`-with-inline-default from the synthesized memberwise init entirely**, which would have made
  every GoPro fixture uncompilable. They're `var`, documented at the field.
- The plan's completeness rule ("one recording number yielding more than one run means a gap")
  under-specified the boundary with `.missingFirstChapter`: a per-run check misfires on a 01+03 set —
  the `[3]` run reads as "missing chapter 01" although chapter 01 sits in the **sibling run**.
  Completeness is decided **per bucket**, and a test pins it.

## Decisions

- **An incomplete GoPro chapter set warns, but still joins** — a user call at G3.4 sign-off that
  **overturns the spec's** "flagged incomplete and not joined". A joined chapters-02..N file is a
  valid, playable, correct MP4, and the corpus holds a real specimen (6338's chapter 01 left the
  archive) a hard block would lock out permanently. Lands opposite to June's index-gap guard, and the
  difference is the point: that guard prevents a **silent corrupt merge** the user can't see; this
  would prevent a **correct join they asked for**. Full entry in `decisions.md`.
- **GoPro chains on timecode; the slack was measured against the right clock.** Full entry in
  `decisions.md`, including the actual-vs-rounded fps rule (1800 frames of 29.97 non-drop is 60.06
  real seconds, not 60.00 — invisible at 25/50/100/200 fps, silently wrong on NTSC rates a Hero 11
  can also shoot).
- **UI: reuse, don't add.** The warning renders through the existing `IntegrityChip` as a new flag
  case — no new view, control or layout. Accepted trade-off: the chip appears once the async
  integrity build finishes, a beat after the row draws.
