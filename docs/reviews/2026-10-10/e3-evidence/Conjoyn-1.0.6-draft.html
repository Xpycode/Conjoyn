What's New in 1.0.6

  • More reliable source scans. Switching folders prevents an older scan from replacing the latest results or adding outdated selections to the queue.
  • Watch-folder recovery. Discovery catches changes that arrive during a scan, retries stalled work, and resumes available watch folders after remounting.
  • Recording-aware history. Watch history distinguishes reused filenames using recording metadata. Ambiguous older history is held for review, with Already processed and Process once choices.
  • Queue recovery. Unreadable saved queues are preserved for recovery. Watch jobs wait until queue and history records can be saved safely.
  • Output collision protection. Queued videos and telemetry sidecars stay paired without replacing existing output files.
  • Recordings search and destination changes. Search is available through Edit → Find. Choosing a destination after restoring a queue offers Apply for pending jobs that still target another folder.

Search filters the visible list and preserves selection. If watch discovery remains stalled because both workers do not return, relaunch conjoyn. Recording identity uses metadata and file descriptors; it cannot distinguish every replacement with identical descriptors.
